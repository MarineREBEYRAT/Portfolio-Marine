NEUMAN PROJECT IMAGE
2024 — 2025 · Academic Project — ESTACA × Neuman

Structural Design of a Catamaran Beam

Naval structures project focused on the structural sizing of a catamaran beam, from the definition of load cases to design validation through numerical simulation.

CAD modelling and static finite element analyses were carried out, together with an optimisation study focusing on the trade-off between weight, structural strength and material selection.

Structural AnalysisFEACADStructural SizingOptimisation
WAVE PROJECT IMAGE
2025 — 2026 · Academic Project — ESTACA × WAVE

Structural Optimisation of a Moth Hull

Project carried out as part of the development of the second version of WAVE's Moth, with the objective of reducing hull weight while maintaining structural strength.

Adaptation of the CAD model using 3DEXPERIENCE, definition of load cases and implementation of a finite element analysis. The project also focused on optimising the hull's composite structure, particularly through the study of laminate thickness and ply orientation, using basalt, cork and epoxy materials.

3DEXPERIENCECADFEAComposite StructuresOptimisation

Hydrodynamic testing carried out in an experimental environment to investigate hull behaviour and its interaction with the surrounding flow.

Wake measurements using resistive probes and an Acoustic Doppler Velocimeter (ADV), together with hull resistance measurements performed in a towing tank.
